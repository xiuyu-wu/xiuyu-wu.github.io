Toys

Small tools I build for research and everyday life.

  • Daily Paper

    A daily arXiv research briefing powered by LLMs, with papers tailored to your interests, in-depth analysis, and collections for saved reads.

  • DailyDisk

    A macOS utility that tracks daily disk usage changes, showing which files and folders grew and where space was freed.

  • Eyes Caller

    A gentle macOS break reminder built around the 20–20–20 rule, with a quiet garden scene that invites you to look away from the screen.
